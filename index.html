03

Digital Relay-Based Protection and Fault Monitoring for EVs

Simulation project · MATLAB / Simulink · Team of 4

Many chargers keep running until something breaks. Mine trips the moment something looks wrong.

  • Simulated a DC-DC fast charger with live voltage and current monitoring.
  • Digital relay logic catches overcurrent, overvoltage and undervoltage.
  • It disconnects the charger, shows the fault code, and waits for a reset.
  • Backed by a literature review and a survey of EV users.
